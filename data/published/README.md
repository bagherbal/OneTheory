# `data/published`

## Purpose

This directory holds immutable, traceable inputs imported from published primary sources.

## Belongs here

Later entries may include group representations, carrier equations, Schoen quotient metadata, bundle topology, published spectrum metadata, and source manifests with hashes and citations.

## Does not belong here

Measured observables, fitted parameters, generated outputs, undocumented transcriptions, speculative native-origin data, or mutable working files do not belong here.

## Dependencies

Concrete model modules may consume declared published inputs through explicit, provenance-preserving interfaces. Published data do not create a bridge to unsupported physical interpretations.

## Promotion condition

An input may be promoted here only when its primary source, transcription, normalization, immutability, hash, and intended scientific scope have been independently checked and reviewed.
