# `conic_pfaffians`

## Purpose

This experiment audits the exact carrier inputs required to reconstruct the
two physical seed Pfaffians for the degree-two conic orbits.

## Belongs here

Input manifests, exact seed embeddings, restricted resolutions, relative-duality
chain-map attempts, determinant-line records, and reproducible boundary reports
belong here until their identities and provenance are independently certified.

## Does not belong here

The stored seven-term or nineteen-term quartics, random restriction matrices,
generic rank witnesses, arbitrary line phases, hidden-bundle guesses, and
unnormalized orbit sums do not belong here as physical results.

## Dependencies

The experiment may inspect production geometry and visible-bundle contracts,
published manifests, and unchanged source artifacts. Production must never
import this experiment or consume its outputs as hidden runtime inputs.

## Promotion condition

Promotion requires explicit seed embeddings, restricted resolutions, certified
relative-duality maps, derived physical quartics, orbit transport, and
determinant-line evidence. Until then the earliest missing input remains open.
