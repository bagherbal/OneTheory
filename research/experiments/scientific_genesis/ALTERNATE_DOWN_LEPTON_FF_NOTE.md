are d seeds `2,4` / e seeds `0,5`. No basis or moduli point is chosen.

literal independently checked inputs. Q and L use their original up/neutrino

archive is consumed with a fixed trusted digest; absent inputs fail before
execution. The common read-only validator replays full constituent equations,

explicitly to the existing coupled-input checker. The old up-Higgs wrapper is
not used. Constant, linear, and quadratic input equations are checked in the
original full differential.

the constant product `P0` and linear product `P1`. An independent ordinary

```text

```

not filled with placeholders. Direct residue, transferred projection, and
inverse-convolution evaluation must agree on the complete actual scalar.

not canonical matter normalization.

Each successful entry is checkpointed immediately with all three full witnesses,
actual input digests, archive-byte snapshots, and this derivation's hash.

changes the mathematical object. A read-only request for an absent entry
