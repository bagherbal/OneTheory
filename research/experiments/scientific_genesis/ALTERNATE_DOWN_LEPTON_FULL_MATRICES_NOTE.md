# Complete remaining holomorphic matrix gate

This assembly is conditional on the same frozen alternate heterotic carrier,
with common determinant repair `(1,2)`, unchanged atlas, original matter bases,
and universal outer basis `(a0,a1)`. It does not select an extension point.

The actual down and charged-lepton mixed blocks have already been independently
checked by full Hom composition, original differential and atlas actions,
exact traces, and literal archive reproduction. Their E-E entries vanish by
the rank-one B exterior relation, not an assigned zero. The remaining input
is sixteen actual parameter-linear F-F coefficients, eight per sector.

The assembler requires all sixteen coefficient metadata files and every full
archive before execution. It also snapshots the sixteen original matter-lift
packets and the actual shared down-Higgs and mixed packets: thirty-four literal
prerequisites in total. Missing prerequisites cause an explicit failure with
no solver or partial-matrix fallback.

Each actual matter lift is independently replayed once in this assembly,
including its full constituent equation, both strict atlas actions, exact
quotient pushout, and coupled identity. The sixteen scalar replays reuse those
verified immutable inputs. Each complete constant product, linear product, and
scalar must equal the corresponding archived witness literally; direct,
transferred, and inverse-convolution traces must agree. The actual down-Higgs
constant and primitive are supplied explicitly and checked in the original
coupled differential. Metadata and archive-byte snapshots must be unchanged
after the full replay. At most two workers schedule these same validators.

Only then may the shared exact one-plus-two-family matrix construction assemble
the two complete 3x3 matrices over Q(omega)[a0,a1]. All mixed entries have degree
zero, while every F-F entry is the actual linear combination of its a0 and a1
coefficients. Every cover trace is divided by nine in the certified quotient
volume frame. A missing coefficient is never replaced by a zero.

The exact polynomial determinant is checked against its separate four-term
expansion. It has degree one, or vanishes identically, because the E-E entry is
zero and each other determinant term contains one F-F entry. The fixed minor
in rows 0,1 and columns 0,1 is nonzero for the already checked mixed inputs,
so each matrix has a rank-two floor. An identically zero determinant remains
a rank-deficient result; the assembler must not force a rank-three locus.

The product of the four actual holomorphic determinants describes their common
rank-three locus on the original projective parameter space. Over this infinite
field a nonzero homogeneous product has a nonempty open complement. No root,
parameter point, basis change, or vacuum choice is needed for this statement.
Metrics and a stabilized common vacuum are nevertheless still required before
physical masses or mixing can be inferred. The matrix node remains unresolved
until all full coefficient replay and independent scalar determinant tests pass.
