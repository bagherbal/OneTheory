## What this does and does not establish

This establishes one complete new-domain section output, conditional on the
certified original lifting and encoding laws. Independent full-cochain and
raw-arrow Gaussian tests attack the functional on all three native mixture
components; exact-point checks use the independently produced original matrix.
Those probe checks are not independent all-column cochain replay, and the
single complete domain is not all fifteen domains or an independent cloud.

The first exact-point cache fill took about 34 seconds versus 22 seconds for
the original evaluator at that point. The completed uncertain-domain run is
not a matched benchmark against the original evaluator on the same domain.
No speedup factor or practical multi-point throughput is asserted. Seventy-four
minutes per complete domain is insufficient evidence for a viable large sampling
campaign. The data identifies a necessary further compression boundary.
