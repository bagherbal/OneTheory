# Original finite lifting functional: transposed evaluation

## Declared dependency edge

This tests `alternate_metric_bounded_support -> alternate_metric_fiber_functionals
-> visible_metrics`, also requiring the certified universal lift formula and
actual uncertain cover frames. It targets complete per-draw section evaluation,
not another bundle classification. Controlled integration, Ricci-flat/HYM
convergence, harmonic metrics and the common vacuum remain separate gates.

## Structural statement

Fix one explicitly declared determinant-certified frame and Reynolds channel.
The existing five-term lifting series, artificial-phase removal, original deck
pullback, Laurent evaluation and quotient projection compose a linear functional
F on the original encoded degree-one cochain space. For an actual encoded residual
r=sum c_b e_b, linearity gives F(r)=sum c_b F(e_b).

An individual e_b need not be closed. No claim `D h'(e_b)=e_b` is made. Closure
and the primitive identity belong to the actual full residual; only evaluation
of the already certified finite linear series is reordered. Object/Koszul
filtration bounds the series even without cancellations or unit closedness.

Outward arithmetic encloses F(e_b) and each c_b. Multiplying and summing those
enclosures contains the original exact F(r), without assuming coefficient or
root independence. Bounds may be wider than the original evaluation order.
No center-zero positive-radius term may be discarded. Cache keys retain the
full original encoded basis label and channel, within one fixed frame. Both
extension parameters share the functional but retain their distinct residuals.

The previous evaluator is retained. Its residual construction and functional
body are factored into reusable methods without changing their arithmetic.
The new evaluator reuses those methods, not a second homotopy, perturbation,
deck or geometry implementation. Every section index and nonsplit coefficient
remains the original one. No physical input, point or parameter is selected.

## Proposed attack and execution boundary

Check exact equality at zero-radius functionals, compare uncertain outputs with
off-center original full-cochain evaluations, exercise zero coordinates and
both parameters/channels, and measure complete original-basis execution on
certified new domains. A cache-hit count alone cannot certify throughput or
physical correctness. Until these gates pass, this is an experimental evaluation
order, not a completed new-domain matrix, independent cloud or metric.
