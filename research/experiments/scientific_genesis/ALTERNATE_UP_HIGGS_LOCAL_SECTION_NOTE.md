# Local contraction of the alternate up-Higgs Hom source

The strict alternate Hom representative has 324 exact terms. Restricting
its right Čech factors to the six affine chart vertices leaves 189 terms,
all in the three syzygy-dual objects. The other 135 terms are on right
fiber overlaps. None of these terms is by itself a tensor Higgs cocycle.

For a chart, let (R) be the (5\times3) pushout relation matrix in row
order ((F0_0,F0_1,F0_2,F0_3,A)). The selected complementary minor is
(D=\det R_{2,3,4}). Its adjugate section (T) satisfies the exact
polynomial identity

\[
R^{\mathsf T}T=D I_3.
\]

The actual mixed outer Hom differential uses object order
((A,F0_0,F0_1,F0_2,F0_3)). Its right-resolution part, independently
assembled from the selected Hilbert–Burch and parent-one extension
arrows, is

\[
B=(R_A^{\mathsf T},-R_{F0_0}^{\mathsf T},\ldots,
   -R_{F0_3}^{\mathsf T}).
\]

For each actual syzygy vector (s), form (v=Ts) in relation-row order
and (m=(v_4,-v_0,-v_1,-v_2,-v_3)) in object order. Then (Bm=Ds)
exactly. There are three nonzero first-factor Čech blocks per right
chart, hence eighteen checked identities. On the principal open (D\ne0),
(m/D) is the explicit local right-resolution primitive. The saved
artifact retains every Laurent coefficient, the denominator, the first
Čech cell, and the Koszul label.

This is only the right-resolution projection of the full differential.
The middle numerators may create left, Čech, or Koszul residuals; the
fiber-overlap terms must still be incorporated, and localized sections
must be compared across minor opens. No global Hom-to-tensor map,
exterior-cone Higgs cocycle, or Yukawa entry follows yet.
