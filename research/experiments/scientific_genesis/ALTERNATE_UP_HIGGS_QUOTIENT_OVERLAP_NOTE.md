# Local quotient image of the alternate Hom fiber overlap

The corrected fiber-overlap middle numerator \(N\) is recorded in Hom
object order \((A,F0_0,\ldots,F0_3)\). Reorder it, including the signs
fixed by the actual right differential, as

\[
v=(-N_{F0_0},-N_{F0_1},-N_{F0_2},-N_{F0_3},N_A).
\]

For the alternate relation \(R\), hypersurface equation \(F\), and
saved Koszul residual \(K\), the exact source identity is
\(R^{\mathsf T}v=F K\). Therefore \(v\) lies in the quotient dual
kernel on the hypersurface, but not necessarily in the ambient kernel.

Let \(J\) be the certified complementary-minor pairing, \(S\) the
sparse inverse numerator for row pair \((0,1)\), and
\(\Delta=J_{01}\). The same minor is the adjugate contraction
denominator \(D\). Define the local quotient numerator \(q=S v\).
The exact prior identity \(J S P=\Delta P\), with
\(P=D I-T R^{\mathsf T}\), gives the cross-multiplied equation

\[
D(Jq-\Delta v)
=F\bigl(J S T K-\Delta T K\bigr).
\]

This is checked directly on the actual \(N,K\) for all three target
base charts. The right-hand correction is retained explicitly. The
quotient numerator has support only in its first two relation rows:
24 exact Laurent terms in each. The other three coordinates vanish.
The homogeneous formula is identical across the checked base charts.

On the principal open \(\Delta\ne0\) and the hypersurface \(F=0\),
\(q/\Delta\) maps under \(J\) to the saved dual class \(v\). This is
a local quotient image, not a global Hom-to-tensor chain map: its
minor-open and base-cover transition comparisons, the exterior-cone
Higgs lift, and all Yukawa entries remain unresolved.
