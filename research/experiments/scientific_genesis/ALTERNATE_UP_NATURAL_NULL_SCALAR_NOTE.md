# Natural quotient evaluation of the actual null channel

This calculation tests the edge from the verified coupled quotient
product to an actual full carrier scalar. It uses the frozen alternate
component, not a measured flavor texture. The evaluator is
`alternate_up_coupled_null_scalar.py`. No result is certified until both
formal coefficients pass all complete differential checks.

## Complete source inputs, not constituent classes alone

For each declared coefficient e_nu, the saved deterministic matter
construction gives E-valued x_nu with

```text
D_E x_nu = - e_nu cup b.
```

Push x_nu through the fixed global signed-minor row q_E to obtain
r_nu in B1. The degree-zero row kills both the A section and the
Hilbert--Burch syzygies. It is the actual map E-to-B1 and needs no
minor inversion. The complete pushout matter is formally

```text
U(a) = b + a0*r_0 + a1*r_1.
```

For the source differential D0+a0*D1+a1*D2, check D0*b=0,
D0*r_nu+D_nu*b=0 and every outer action on an r_mu is zero.
The last statement follows from the outer row vanishing on B;
there is no uncomputed quadratic source correction. The constituent
b alone is not silently treated as a closed pushout representative.

## Formal degree of the product and Higgs evaluation

The quotient has K=B1 tensor I6 B2 and exterior-F blocks. Its product
is W-N_alpha-N_beta+M. The inner correction on a B-supported input
lands in the killed A wedge B relation. Both outer corrections on
such an input land in B wedge B or vanish because no row leaves B.
The ordinary product of two B corrections also vanishes. Therefore,
for arbitrary actual b and r satisfying these support conditions,

```text
P_Q(U_L(a), U_R(a)) = P0 + a0*P1_0 + a1*P1_1,
P0 in exterior F; each P1_nu in K.
```

This is a support proof of formal degree, not an interpolation fit.
Regression attacks include even and odd source objects over a
nonrational exact coefficient. They test the theorem but do not
replace its reason. No a=1 extension point is selected: each
coefficient presentation is used to evaluate its homogeneous term.

Use the existing full quotient Higgs
H(a)=h_K+a0*kappa_0+a1*kappa_1. Each kappa is decoded from its
explicit content-addressed archive, then retargeted to the declared
exterior-F block. The constant h has only K support. Validate the
full dual identities D0*h=0, D0*kappa_nu+D_nu*h=0 and the absence
of outer actions on kappa before forming a scalar.

Consequently the evaluation is exactly

```text
S(a) = a0*(h_K cup P1_0 + kappa_0 cup P0)
     + a1*(h_K cup P1_1 + kappa_1 cup P0).
```

Neither a constant nor a quadratic scalar is suppressed by a
coefficient truncation: their matrix block compositions are zero.
The full product identities and scalar differential are checked
before tracing either coefficient. Generic Hom evaluation tests
include mixed arrows, polynomial syzygies and every Koszul pair.

## Trace and remaining scientific scope

The cover trace assigns one to the ordered ambient k2 H5 generator:
all Laurent exponents minus one, with cell (012,012,01). The evaluator
compares the literal coefficient with the transferred scalar residue
only after full closure. No ninefold quotient factor is inserted.
No matter metric, Higgs metric or stabilized vacuum is supplied.

Earlier ordered scalar screens are comparison witnesses only. The
new scalar is obtained from complete inputs and the corrected product;
its difference from the earlier screen is computed, never set to zero.
A nonzero new cover residue would still require the determinant/Higgs
identification, equivariant descent and explicit trace convention
before assignment to a quotient matrix. A zero residue would instead
change the rank-three gate. Neither outcome is preselected.

The complete 3-by-3 matrix, normalized Yukawas and low-energy observables
remain distinct required results. Computing one null-channel scalar
does not count as finishing any of them.
