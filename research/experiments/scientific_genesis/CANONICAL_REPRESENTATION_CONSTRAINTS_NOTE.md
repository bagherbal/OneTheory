# Canonical relations require representations, not labels

## Meaningful foundational edge

This necessary-condition audit concerns a claimed exact canonical quantum
realization on the Genesis-to-UV path. It does not derive quantum postulates,
causality, gravity, or the selected heterotic theory. The current quantum API
accepts named finite Hilbert-space and canonical-relation **declarations**;
these do not construct operator matrices or certify their relations.

## Universal finite-dimensional obstruction

Let A and B be endomorphisms of the same n-dimensional vector space over a
commutative characteristic-zero field, with n > 0. In any common basis,

    tr(AB) = sum_i sum_j A_ij B_ji
           = sum_j sum_i B_ji A_ij = tr(BA).

Thus tr(AB-BA) = 0. If AB-BA = lambda I and lambda != 0, its trace would also
equal n lambda != 0, a contradiction. This proof covers every finite dimension
and every pair of endomorphisms, including complex coefficients; it is not an
inference from the saved small witnesses. It is invariant under a common basis
change. No physical value of hbar or other dimensional constant is needed.

For the explicit polynomial vector space with basis (1,t,...,t^(n-1)), take D
to be differentiation and X to be multiplication by t followed by truncation.
Direct application to every basis element gives

    DX-XD = I - n P_last.

The boundary defect is nonzero in characteristic zero. D is NOT a derivation
of the algebra K[t]/(t^n) in that characteristic: differentiating t^n yields
n t^(n-1). These finite operators are algebraic witnesses, not a declared
physical oscillator, a matter state or a convergence certificate. Infinite
operators have domain issues and need not admit the finite trace argument;
controlled finite approximations are not ruled out by this exact obstruction.

## Deliberate counterexamples prevent overclaiming

In characteristic p, differentiation DOES descend to F_p[t]/(t^p), since the
derivative of t^p is zero. The same matrices obey DX-XD = I in dimension p:
the boundary defect p P_last vanishes. Examples at p=2,3,5 are saved to attack
any omission of the characteristic-zero premise. This is not a privileged
physical role for three or a complex quantum realization.

There is no unital scalar-ring map F_p -> C (or any characteristic-zero field).
Such a map would send 0=p*1 to p*1 != 0. This rules out exact scalar arithmetic
relabeling, not nonlinear emergence, a continuum limit, or a different theory.
No adapter implements this nonexistent identification.

Finite quantum structure is NOT excluded in general. The two-by-two algebraic
matrices C=[[0,1],[0,0]] and E=[[0,0],[1,0]] satisfy C^2=E^2=0 and CE+EC=I.
This exact CAR witness defeats an overbroad claim that all finite quantum
representations are impossible. No particle, vacuum or physical coefficient is
constructed by the witness.

## Independent checks and limits

Regression tests reconstruct products entry by entry using independent
Fraction-pair arithmetic and direct modular integer sums, alongside the
existing based-map and prime-field engines. They attack nonzero and zero
right-hand sides, both supported characteristic-zero fields, basis mismatches,
positive characteristic and the CAR counterexample. The analytic proof above
is the universal certificate; witness coverage alone is not that proof.

The result constrains exact canonical representability only. Quantum phase,
Lorentzian causality, gravitational coupling, constants and UV structure remain
assumed or unresolved as recorded in the scientific graph. It supplies no
physical Yukawa normalization, metric, hidden bundle, vacuum or observable.
