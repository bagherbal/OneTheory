## Full-space proof

Let S_i have fiber rank r in the complete N-dimensional section space, w_i>0,
and H=C C† be any positive form. Set F_i=S_i C. Then

`P_i = F_i† (F_i F_i†)^-1 F_i`

is an orthogonal rank-r projector. No section has been discarded. In the
declared scalar gauge, projective balance requires

`B(H) = (N/r) sum_i beta_i P_i = I`, `beta_i = w_i / sum_j w_j`.

Its trace is N. Every summand is positive semidefinite. On the image of P_i,
the Rayleigh quotient of B is at least `(N/r)*beta_i`. Consequently every
atomic multiplier must be at most one. If q=(N/r)*beta_i>1, then for every H:

`||B-I||_op >= q-1`, `||B-I||_F^2/N >= (r/N)*(q-1)^2`.

The second inequality uses the r eigenvalues above q supplied by the min-max
principle. These statements are invariant under a full invertible section
basis change and the unitary freedom in C. They are not coordinate truncation,
an approximate eigenvalue certificate, or a change in physical normalization.

condition proves neither span, stability nor existence of a fixed point.
