## Independent exact intersection test

Explicitly order divisor coordinates as `tau1=x`, `tau2=u`, `phi=p`. In the
ambient Chow ring of `P2_x × P2_u × P1_p`, the cover has class
`(3x+p)(3u+p)`. A cover triple product is the coefficient of `x²u²p` in the
three divisor factors times that class. This calculation does not read the
production intersection tensor's entries. It independently reproduces all
five coefficients of each published homogeneous quadratic, then agrees with
the production tensor and explicit ninefold quotient normalization.

At `L`, the nine **rank-one line slopes on the cover**, in source order, are
`1296, -3600, -2088, 648, -4248, -432, 1080, 1728, -3816`.
Quotient slopes are precisely one ninth of these. Candidate line classes in
the sufficient proof are not automatically determinants of actual subsheaves;
no unspecified rank factor is treated as a measured subbundle slope.

On the mathematical slice `(14,16,y)`, the inequalities reduce exactly to
`17/5 < y < 51`. The lower limiting bound is the line class `(1,-4,2)`;
the upper is `(2,-2,-1)`. Endpoints fail the strict test. For example `y=4`
passes the sufficient test, but this is not a selected physical Kähler class,
new section system, or vacuum. The retained `y=1` fails four inequalities.
Every source polynomial is homogeneous of degree two. Hence no positive
rescaling, including any integral power of the current line twist, can make
the existing sufficient theorem apply to its ray.

## What this does not prove

The paper explicitly distinguishes sufficient curved bounds from the actual
stability boundary. Failing them does **not** prove instability. In particular
the mandatory rank-two constituent with determinant `(-2,2,0)` has negative
slope here: the displayed determinant-line value is `-432`, while its actual
rank-two cover slope is `-216`. No destabilizing subsheaf is constructed.
The old finite-cloud atomic obstruction remains solely an empirical-measure
no-go. All old and new samples, streams, roots, frames and sections are retained;
the live new population is not stopped by this theorem-scope test.

The [generalized Donaldson analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/1103.3041), section
3.2, distinguishes finite-twist balance, Gieseker stability, slope stability,
the large-twist limit and background/line untwisting. A stability certificate
at `(6,9,3)` cannot silently certify an HYM limit on the current twist's ray.
Conversely, using the retained sections as an ansatz for a separately declared
background is not ruled out here; it requires its own derived algorithm and
compatible convergence/untwisting, not a change of labels.

The next physical dependency is actual stability for the intended
polarization and a compatible controlled background metric. Neither more
samples nor finite balance alone supplies it. No Ricci-flat metric, HYM
connection, matter/Higgs metric, normalized Yukawa, stabilized common vacuum,
or Genesis-to-heterotic derivation is claimed by this exact scope check.
