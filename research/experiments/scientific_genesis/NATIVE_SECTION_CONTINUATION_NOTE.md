# Retained native roots and complete original trial integrands

## Scope

Root admission is not frame or integrand admission. This controller composes
the existing native root continuation, `UncertainCoverPoint`, original
`BoundedFiberFrame`, complete `CompiledColumn.evaluate` archive consumer and
full `contract_columns` operation. None of their mathematical algorithms is
replaced. The former complete consumer was pinned to another single regression
frame; it could not consume a retained-root continuation request.

The ordered global section identity belongs to the original 5,345 cochains,
not to the numeric stream at any point. The new computation retains that
identity, all constant/a0/a1 coefficients and the same named free generators.
The explicit unit-H input is a computational trial form, not a physical or
canonical Hermitian metric. Extension parameters remain symbolic.

## Retention and named-frame contract

The caller declares the chart, ordered relation pivot rows, center precision,
residue scale and covering degree. Refinement may increase precision but may
not change any of these other choices. Native root continuation first verifies
every exposed prefix and matches the new disks to the retained parent. A root
failure retains the last admitted root and frame. A successful root followed
by a chart, relation-minor or weight failure retains the successful root and
the last admitted frame. Neither event licenses a new address or a new basis.
If only the frame calculation is retried with an identical address and root
policy, the existing certified root is retained without proposing new disks.
Equal-radius disks need not be strictly inside themselves; no branch matching
or resampling is required for reuse of the identical native certificate.

The declared execution uses the original A regression address, levels 8 then
12, and an intervening one-cell cap failure. The later spacing/phase prefixes
extend 32 to 48 bits. Root selectors are already terminated; native disk
containment, not their newly sorted indices, determines the continued branch.
The fixed cover chart is (0,0,0), quotient rows are (0,2) and (0,1,2).
The explicit computational residue scale is one; the published covering degree
is nine. These retain the existing declared research normalization, not a
derived physical volume normalization or a choice made from observables.

## Conditional eventual admission and convergence

For a limiting input outside the already proved proper root and named-frame
failure loci, native root refinement eventually yields disks for the same
simple roots with radii tending to zero. Homogeneous input coordinates and
their errors converge. The normalized cover pivots are nonzero at the limit,
so their inverses and normalized coordinate bounds converge as well.

Each original relation coefficient is a finite Laurent expression whose poles
are confined to the declared nonzero chart pivots. The two relation minors are
nonzero on the full-measure named-frame locus. Adjugate division therefore
eventually excludes zero and its circular enclosures converge. The fixed
quotient projection coefficients converge without any extension-parameter
specialization. Increasing center precision with the input bound precision
prevents a fixed arithmetic error floor.

There are finitely many original sections and finite polynomial terms in the
completed coefficient-ring archive. Polynomial evaluation followed by the
original projection is a finite composition of continuous additions,
multiplications and admitted divisions. Its outward dyadic rounding errors
tend to zero. Consequently every one of the 64,140 constant/a0/a1 coefficient
enclosures converges. Finiteness makes their maximum radius tend to zero;
there is no need to assume convergence from a handful of section probes.

For any explicitly fixed positive section form with finite entries, the
existing full contraction is another finite expression in those coefficients
and their conjugates. All nine trial covariance blocks therefore converge.
On the selected smooth cover the homogeneous conormal denominator is positive;
its weight enclosure eventually admits division and converges. Multiplying
the full covariance blocks by that weight preserves coefficient convergence.
The resulting parameter-polynomial trial integrands converge uniformly on
each fixed compact affine extension-parameter set, because only nine monomials
eta_m conjugate(eta_n) occur. No parameter point is selected in this argument.

This gives conditional eventual finite admission and coefficient convergence,
not a uniform rate over inputs near excluded loci, an expected-work bound,
an independent cloud, a certified stopping tolerance or an integration error.
The finite execution records its actual radii rather than inferring that any
requested numerical accuracy was attained. The full inverse kernel, metric
iteration, untwisting, Ricci-flat/HYM convergence and common stabilized vacuum
are separate requirements. Genesis-to-UV also remains unresolved.
