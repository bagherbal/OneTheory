## Interpretation and cost decision

A smaller full-population reference functional would support, but not establish,
continuum metric improvement. A larger value rejects an improvement claim on
this population and background; it does not imply continuum bundle instability.
The finite-atomic balance obstruction still forbids treating further H2 steps
on this same measure as convergence. No H2 is executed. A timing probe of the
actual full-factor action must precede the complete job; all old/fresh sampling
workers and their inputs are retained, not restarted or replaced.

The actual sample-zero timing probe completed: the full 16-row triangular
first-jet action took 0.320 seconds with two BLAS threads. The eight independent
tests passed in 115.99 seconds including source/array inspection and compilation.
The factor action adds roughly eleven minutes across 2048 points at this measured
rate, beyond geometry evaluation; this is a cost estimate, not a completion ETA.
The complete experiment is therefore reachable without replacing its population
by a cheaper subset. Run with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2 OMP_NUM_THREADS=2` through
`python -u -m research.experiments.scientific_genesis.nonunit_connection_curvature`.

Neither outcome certifies roundoff, sampling error, a Ricci-flat background,
HYM convergence, determinant-volume normalization, matter/Higgs metrics,
canonical Yukawas or a common stabilized vacuum. Old validation is not blind.
The fresh 16384-point population remains the distinct next sampling refinement.
