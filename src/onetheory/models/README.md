`standard_model.py` holds the low-energy gauge representations, charges, Higgs representation, and exact B-L extension used by the carrier. `heterotic_schoen` will hold only published carrier data and calculations directly attached to that carrier, including geometry, visible and hidden sectors, flavor, metrics, instantons, consistency, and vacuum work.

## Does not belong here

Native-origin interpretations, generation shortcuts, tested Wilson-line dead ends, superseded two-Higgs data, witness quartics, excluded hidden-bundle routes, generic bridge interfaces, and terminal observations used for upstream selection do not belong here.

## Dependencies

Models may use core, math, physics, and sibling model data. They must not import engine, reality, verification, research, tests, or observations as selectors.

## Promotion condition

Research may be promoted only into its true concrete owner after direct physical derivation, provenance, certificate or convergence evidence, review, and unit and scientific tests establish the object without a fabricated fallback.
