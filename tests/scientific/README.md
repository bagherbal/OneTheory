# Scientific tests

## Purpose

This directory will test promoted physical claims, carrier calculations, certificates, convergence evidence, and falsification conditions at their declared scope.

## Belongs here

Tests for published inputs, exact OneTheory results, controlled numerical outputs, direct physical compositions, unresolved-state propagation, and terminal observable comparisons belong here after implementation.

## Does not belong here

Measured data used to select geometry, guessed coefficients, synthetic universes, unreviewed hypotheses, speculative bridges, and assertions that open components exist do not belong here.

## Dependencies

Scientific tests may inspect production and use declared data manifests. Production remains independent of tests, observations, and research experiments.

## Promotion condition

Research may be promoted only after provenance, exactness or convergence evidence, an independent certificate, scientific review, and a test at the same stated scope are available.
